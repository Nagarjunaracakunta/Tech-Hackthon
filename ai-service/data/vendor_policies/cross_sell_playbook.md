# Cross-Sell Playbook

- Only offer cross-sell products to applicants who are approved on the spot
  with a risk score of 85 or higher — a strong lead is the best moment to
  introduce a second product, not a borderline one.
- Term Life and Health Cover are the two products with the highest
  attach-rate for approved motor-insurance leads in this segment.
- Do not cross-sell to any applicant flagged with `adverse_prior_history` or
  `image_mismatch`, even if their numeric score later recovers — resolve the
  flag first.
