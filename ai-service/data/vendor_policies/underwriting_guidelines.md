# Motor Insurance Underwriting Guidelines (v1)

- Applicants with a declared credit score below 500 are high risk and should
  be routed to manual review unless offset by a clean multi-year insurance
  history with no lapses or fraud flags.
- A prior insurance history that mentions "lapsed", "denied", or "fraud"
  requires manual underwriter sign-off regardless of the computed risk score.
- Vehicle photo image-match confidence below 50% is an automatic reject —
  treat it as a potential misrepresented-vehicle case.
- Vehicle photo image-match confidence between 50% and 70% requires manual
  review even if the numeric risk score is otherwise low.
- Missing any of the four required documents (identity, credit history,
  vehicle document, vehicle photo) blocks automatic approval; route to
  review with the specific missing items named.
